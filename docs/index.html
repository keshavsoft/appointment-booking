KeshavSoft CareCall Suite

Hospital Intake, Slot Booking & Token Allocation

A lightweight, modular, and flat-file friendly telephone intake application. Captures patient phone bookings, allocates tokens into 30-minute doctor slots, and manages physical arrival check-in.
